Transitous needs donations to keep its server infrastructure running in the long term. FOSSGIS e.V. is a non-profit association based in Germany. Besides Transitous, it supports other open GIS projects such as OpenStreetMap, which in turn benefit Transitous and the wider community.

Donations in kind (e.g. servers) can sometimes help us as well. Please get in touch with us (e.g. via Matrix) if you have something to offer.

Please include at least the word "donation" in the payment reference. Please avoid earmarking your donation for a specific purpose without consulting us first. Earmarked donations create extra work, as we must ensure the money is spent as intended. This can be tricky when a project has already ended or needs less than expected. For donation receipts in Germany, see the main FOSSGIS e.V. donation page.
